# Notes
-  While flight refers to the operation in the aircraft in the air, the mission is a much broader concept. it refers to the main objective, the bigger picture behind the flight.
- a mission can involve one or more flights.
- The take-off is only an aspect of the flight during which the aircraft lifts off from the ground.
