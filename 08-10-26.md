# Notes
- the remote pilot is the one who has been assigned by the operator to control an RPA during flight.
- the operator is different from the remote pilot. the operator is the individual or organisation responsible for conducting or offering to conduct an aerial operation.
- another related term is the remote pilot-in-command, who has the final authority in ensuring the safe conduction of aerial operations.
- the term 'aircraft' by definition includes both manned and unmanned machines that fly.
- the payload is not every single thing the aircraft carries on board. it only refers to the equipment specifically required for the accomplishment of the mission, which is not otherwise required for the basic operation of the aircraft. it doesn't include things like the airframe or propellers - it is only mission equipment.
