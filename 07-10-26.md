# Notes
- 'Drone' is a common informal term referring to any unmanned flying vehicle.
- A UAV is the specific technical term that means Unmanned Aerial Vehicle.
- UAS is the entire system behind aerial operations, which the UAV is only a part of.
- UAS involves a UAV, a ground station, C2-link (or command-and-control link) and other systems.
- RPAS means Remotely-Piloted Aerial System and is a specific term under UAS in which the UAV has a remote pilot controlling the aircraft from a distance.
- Aircrafts operating within this system are referred to more specifically as Remotely-Piloted Aircrafts (RPA).
- When an RPA is in the air, communication between the aircraft and the remote pilot is done through a command and control link.
