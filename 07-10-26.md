# Notes
- UAV stands for Unmanned Aerial Vehicle and is often commonly and informally referred to as a drone. UAS is entire system involved in aerial missions, including the UAV, the ground station and others. RPAS is a specific type of UAS in which the UAV is controlled from a distance by a remote pilot. While the term 'drone' is often used informally to referred to unmanned flying vehicles, RPAS is a technical, more specific term referring to remotely piloted aircrafts.
