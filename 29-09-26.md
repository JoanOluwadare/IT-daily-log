# Notes 
- The AeroOps platform is the foundation of everything else that exists in the ecosystem. The platform has various functionalities and features that characterise its role as the central hub of the ecosystem, such as a database management system that prevents data from one company to leak into another and role-based access control that determines what role a user can access upon login.
- The web app provides a kind of lightweight alternative to the desktop app, but won't give access to all the functionalities that the desktop app gives.
- AeroOps is designed for enterprise clients who need to perform quick tasks without needing the full Desktop App or being at their work station.
- The web app will be developed in phases, starting with the Client Portal. It's designed to be compatible with the latest versions of common web browsers. 
- Clients can't view data outside their organisation or projects. Administrators can modify user accounts and assign roles from their dashboard.
