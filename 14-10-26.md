# Notes 
- Airspace isn't just empty sky. It is an organised operating environment shared by different aircraft. It's like an organised highway system in the sky.
- Different types of airspace incude: Controlled, Uncontrolled, Segregated, Restricted and Prohibited. 
- Controlled has regulations, and describes a service arrangement. Uncontrolled is not automaticaly free of restrictions. Segregated is limited to specific operations or users, for example, an area limited to just military operations. Restricted has particular rules for operations, but doesn't limit to one user or type of operation like Segregated does. Prohibited is banned completely operations except there's an authorised exception. 
- Geofence is a technical control, while airspace compliance is an operational and regulatory responsibility. 
- Even with geofence configured and requirements met, the remote pilot is expected to monitor the operation. 
- an incident is not an accident. It doesn't necessarily mean that damage has occured. 
- An incident is aslo different from a hazard. while an incident could affect or affects safety, a hazard has the potential to cause harm.
