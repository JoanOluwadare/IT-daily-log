# Notes
- regulatory/overview.md outlines the regulatory strategies incorporated in the AeroOps system. 
- Compliance with aviation regulations and authorities has always factored into the design of AeroOps, and is not a last-minute addition.
- Aviation policies in African countries vary, so the AeroOps system is built to be adaptable across different landscapes., with specific target on the Nigerian, South African and Kenyan markets.
- AeroOps integrates with OpenUTM, a UTM service so that AeroOps can ensure that drone operations are conducted safely and in compliance with airspace rules.
- The integration will be between AeroOps backend engine and OpenUTM's backend engine, Flight Blender, and the connection will only be server-to-server.
- UTM communication will be synced with major stages and workflows of mission, from pre-flight planning to post-flight.
- POST, GET, DELETE are the primary API endpoints used.
- The Data Mapping section maps the different but corresponding terminologies between AeroOps and OpenUTM to each other for clarity, compliance and ease of integration.
