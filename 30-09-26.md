# Notes
-  The transition of AeroOps' MVP to advanced and AI-driven version is a planned evolution. The MVP focuses on delivering core functionalities. product/roadmap.md gives a detailed sequential account of these intentional upgrades. 
-  The second phase is designed to improve planning quality and data capture by adding live weather sources, regional airspace updates, Companion Mode between the mobile and Desktop apps, and other enhancements for the desktop app.
-  In phase 3, AI becomes more proactive and predictive than reactive. AI also generates insight, risk reports and maintenance alerts.
-  In subsequent phases, the system will be opened up for third-party integration, with stronger AI capabilities.
-  The product/target-users.md document gives an overview of the primary and secondary users of the Hub, their key struggles and the solutions brought by the platform.
-  AeroOPs is designed for drone professionals, as well as other enterprises that aren't drone professionals but still use drones in performing their professional services.
