# Notes
- An aircraft must be confirmed fit for operation before flight takes place. 
- Checklists for pre-flight inspection depends on the aircraft model and operating procedure but may include:
- Airframe and structure, propellers and motors, battery and power, payload and attachments, control and navigation systems. 
- Pre-flight preparation is different from pre-flight inspection. Pre-flight preation is broader and can include inspection, reviewing weather, assessing risk, mission plaaning and more. 
- Post-flight inspection may include airframe, propellers and motors, battery, payload, system messages and flight records. 
- Information from inspection may trigger maintenance, defect reports or further technical investigation. 
- The workflow is Maintenance - Pre-flight inspection - Flight operation - Post-flight inspection - Maintenance, if required. 
- Pre-flight and post-flight are associated with individual flights. Maintenance can be scehduled independently. 
- Maintenance is ongoing service and repair over an aircraft's service life.
