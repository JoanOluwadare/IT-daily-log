# Notes 
- Aerial work is an aircraft operation in which an aircraft is used for specialised work. 
- Air traffic means all aircraft in flight or operating on the manoeuvring area of an aerodrome. 
- No RPAS operator is to operate without a hazard identification system as approved by the NCAA. 
- basic risk assessment process: identify the hazard, assess potential consequences, evaluate the risk, choose risk controls, reassess` the remaining risk, document and review. 
- Risk mitigation doesn't remove the hazard itself; it reduces the probability of encountering the hazard or limits the consequences if something goes wrong. 
- Risk can be assessed in 2 dimensions: Likelihood & severity. 
- A risk matrix combines both parameters to help classify the risk.
- Maintenance is work carried out to keep an aircraft out to keep an aircraft and its associated equipment in a safe, serviceable operating condition, or to restore that condition when a fault or defect occurs. 
- Maintenance can include inspection, cleaning, repair, replacement. 
- There's Scheduled maintenance and Unscheduled maintenance. 
- Maintenance may involve the airframe, propeller, motors, batteries, flight contrller, navigation equipment, communication equipment, remote pilot station and payload interfaces. 
Maintenance is also a documentation activity.
